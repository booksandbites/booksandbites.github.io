Our approach

Food is part of the foundation.

The Bites initiative connects our literacy work with community care. We coordinate surplus meal pickups from restaurant partners and deliver them to local shelters on a weekly basis — free to both sides.

Every meal we deliver is one less thing standing between a person and their potential.

How it works

  • Restaurants donate surplus food at the end of service
  • Our volunteers coordinate same-day pickup
  • Meals are delivered to partner shelters within hours
  • Shelters log recipients so we can track our impact
  • We report outcomes back to partners monthly
