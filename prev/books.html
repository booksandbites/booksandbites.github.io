Literacy Initiative

📚 Books

Curated reading lists, guided literacy lessons, and free resources for every skill level — from the very first letter to full fluency.

What we do

What is the Books initiative?

Books is our core literacy program — a structured journey through reading, from the alphabet all the way to deep comprehension. Every resource is free, judgment-free, and built with real learners in mind.

We partner with educators and community organizations to ensure our materials reach the people who need them most.

Who it's for

Every learner is welcome.

Books is designed for adult learners returning to education, children beginning their reading journey, and anyone who wants to strengthen their literacy foundation.

You don't need any prior knowledge — just a willingness to learn.

Skill levels

Where will you start?

Our resources are organized into three tracks so you can jump in at the right place.

Beginner
🌱

Just Starting Out

Letters, sounds, and your very first words. No experience required whatsoever.

Intermediate
📗

Building Fluency

Sight words, sentence structure, and reading with growing confidence and speed.

Advanced
🏆

Deep Comprehension

Strategies for understanding, analyzing, and engaging deeply with any text.
