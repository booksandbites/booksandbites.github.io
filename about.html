Our story

Why we do this work.

Education should never have a price tag. We're a small nonprofit on a big mission.

Who we are

We are a nonprofit organization dedicated to providing free, high-quality literacy education to learners of all ages and backgrounds. We believe that reading and writing are fundamental rights — and that every person deserves access to the tools that make them possible.

Our team is made up of educators, volunteers, and community members who give their time because they believe in the mission.

Our values

Access for all

No cost, no barriers. Every lesson is free and always will be.

Dignity first

Learning at any age is brave. We create judgment-free spaces.

Community rooted

We build with communities, not just for them.

Continuous growth

New lessons every month, always improving what we offer.
